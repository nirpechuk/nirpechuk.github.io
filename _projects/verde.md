Verde helps people report environmental issues, organize local events, and coordinate action. Agents pull and cluster public data to surface issues, while the community can verify reports, join cleanups, and earn points for their impact. Built in under 24 hours at HackMIT 2025.

**Built with:** Flutter, Supabase, multimodal AI.
