Truely uses a multi-agent pipeline to inspect a page’s live DOM, investigate claims with web search, and annotate misinformation and deepfakes in place with citations. Built at the UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2025, where it won Letta’s Most Creative Usage of Letta award.

**Built with:** TypeScript, Svelte, Letta, LLM agents.
