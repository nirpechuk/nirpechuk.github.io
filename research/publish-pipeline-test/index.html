How views are counted

A view is a page load. A unique viewer is a browser counted once per page per UTC day. Research views count only after unlocking. Clearing browser storage or using another browser can count someone again; blocked storage omits them from unique counts. Daily uniques cannot be added together to get unique people for the whole period.

History starts September 27, 2026, when daily tracking was introduced. Earlier visits remain in lifetime totals only. These are approximate counts from a public counter service; blockers, outages, and automated visits can affect them.
