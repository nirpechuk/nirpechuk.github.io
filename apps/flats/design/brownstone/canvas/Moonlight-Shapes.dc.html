How the patch is projected

left window, worked
  1. Project with P(X,Y,Z) = [195 + X·s, 280 + (1.92 − Y)·s], s = 62.5·5 / (5 − Z).
  2. Sill edge lands at Z 0.70 (y 419.5); head edge at Z 1.90 (y 473.5).
  3. Slide X right ≈ 0.35 at the near edge and ≈ 0.75 at the far edge: the moon is high and up-left. Past X 2.0 the patch folds up the right wall.
  4. Treatments only trim this shape: curtains trim X, blinds trim the far edge, café curtains the near edge, shutters stripe it across.
