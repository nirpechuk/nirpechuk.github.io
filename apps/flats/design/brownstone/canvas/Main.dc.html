Brownstone

One building, one night street. Every workout makes your window glow a little warmer and your flat feel a little more like home.

Read the Brownstone design spec
Principle 1
The window light stays
Cold moonlight is the one constant. It goes from the main light to a quiet accent, never away.
Principle 2
Every lamp is a light
Light items carry a position, radius and warmth. Placing one visibly lights its corner.
Principle 3
Warmer as it fills
Darkness and cool tint ease off with cosiness: blue and bare on day one, amber when homey.
Principle 4
Nothing is ever lost
Quiet weeks dim the lamps toward candlelight. One workout turns every one back on.
