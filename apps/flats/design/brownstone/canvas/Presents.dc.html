Presents — tiers and unboxing

Every logged workout earns a present. The wrapping tells you how good a week it was before you open it, and opening it should feel like a lamp coming on.

Tier by workouts that week
1 → Tier 1 2–3 → Tier 2 4+ → Tier 3
Each present offers CHOICES_PER_PRESENT = 3; you keep one.

Unboxing a Tier 3 hatboxfour beats · about 2.5 seconds · one tap to open, one to choose
CHOICES_PER_PRESENT = 3
1 · Closed
Waits in the room’s own light. Tap the lid.
2 · Lid lifts
Warm light spills out and lights the room.
3 · Three choices
Tissue parts; three Tier 3 items fan out.
4 · Place it
Your pick, in its palette. Place it opens decorate mode.
The rule: tier comes from workouts logged that week — 1 → Tier 1 kraft parcel · 2–3 → Tier 2 ribbon box · 4+ → Tier 3 hatbox. Seasonal and surprise wraps can dress any tier. A slow week still gets a present.
